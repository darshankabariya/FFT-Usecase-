Image to CNC toolpath

Drop a PNG or JPG, set the size and the cutter, check the preview, download the G-code. Everything runs in this page; the picture never leaves your device.

Picture
Outlines: dark shapes become closed cutting loops, holes included. Best for logos, lettering, silhouettes.
Size and origin
The whole picture scaled to this width; the design inside it comes out at the size shown under the preview.
Tracing
Pixels darker than this are the design. Auto uses Otsu's method.
0 = off. 30 to 80 rounds pixel stair-steps away; good for organic shapes, bad for sharp corners.
The line visits every dot once. 2000 to 4000 is a good range; more is slower to compute and to cut.
Cutter

The tool runs along the drawn line, like an engraver or V-bit. There is no cutter-radius offset and no pocketing: a cut-out that must fall out at true size needs offsetting in a CAM program.

cutrapid (tool up)design as traced
G-code

Output is plain GRBL dialect: G21 G90 G17 G94, G0 rapids at safe Z, G1 cuts, M3/M5, M30. Z0 is the top of the stock. The file downloads as .txt; rename it to .nc or .gcode if your sender insists, every common sender (UGS, Candle, bCNC, cncjs) opens it either way.
